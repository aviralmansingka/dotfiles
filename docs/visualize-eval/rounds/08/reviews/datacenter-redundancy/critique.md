## Source corrections and visual findings

- **Pins — satisfied, not a blocker.** The human requires the border and rack leaves at the same height and the spines above them (source original line 248). v3 spine labels sit at row 20, their boxes at rows 18–22; all four BL/Leaf labels sit at row 32. Six nodes are pinned with zero conflicts. One must not unpin or flip edges simply to reduce crossings.
- **Fabric bridges and detours — high defect; near-side reverse-edge routing is an unproven capability blocker.** v3 shows 14 bridge glyphs and five reported touching bends; all views total 18 and eight, respectively. The four BL→spine historical arrows go against the pinned tier direction and are routed around/above the spine boxes; that source direction and geometry force *some* non-monotone routing, not necessarily these particular lengthy loops or all 14 bridges. The evidence does not prove an optimum or that 14 are unavoidable. For example, outer wraps travel to row 14 and below row 35 around the other tier; a shorter near-side spine entry is a plausible alternative but not shown as achievable in the frozen renderer. Treat the current unreadability and route economy as defects; the renderer's ability to route such heads near-side is a reported capability gap, not an independently proved impossibility. Touching bends alone are not crossings.
- **Label ownership — high defect and unresolved capability issue.** v1/v2/v3 checks flag two owners each; v4 flags zero. On v1 at columns 35 and 37 the lanes leave adjacent MUX ports only two cells apart; `x-connect` at [26,30,9,1] ends immediately left of one lane while another label is crowded into the gap. v2 adjacent solid/dashed runs at columns 33/35 and repeated `active`/`standby?`, especially rows 20–24 and 32–39, invite wrong association. v3 ECMP labels at [37,23,4,1], [36,24,4,1] and [41,27,4,1] are surrounded by sibling corridors. These are observable ambiguity examples; the checker does not identify its exact six owners. The generator records a structural explanation and an lr test, but does not prove there is no other unambiguous layout that preserves all constraints: v2 lr removes ambiguity but reports 80×19, over budget; v4 lr succeeds. Therefore no exception is justified merely by its structural claim.
- **Regions — high defect, suspected renderer capability blocker.** The v1 `wan`, v2 `core`, v3 rack-leaf (but with its title broken), and v4 `rack` are the only boxes wrapping their members; remaining declared groups collapse into detached, empty slivers. - **Family evidence — present, not a workflow omission.** `generation.json` contains the line verbatim: “highest density in the case set (18 nodes, 28 links, every tier cross-connected 4-way so two links land on each double-arrowhead target), and the source forbids motion ('remove the dots flowing for now'), which is this family's static signature.” The dominant family is right, though “every tier cross-connected 4-way” overstates the ISP→MUX tier, which has four links total with two ISPs per MUX, not all-to-all.
