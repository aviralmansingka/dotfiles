## Detailed findings and required qualifiers

**F1/F2 — REGION geometry, high defect.** On the 83×51 ANSI capture, the v1 `workloads` region spans x=17–66, y=7–12 and encloses its workloads. The intended `virtual nets` region instead spans x=70–72, y=21–26, while its two members are at x=23–42 and 46–61, y=22–25. The intended `homelab host` region is x=67–69, y=41–46, while the host node is x=20–64, y=42–45. These are three-column slivers outside their members, not containers. Their titles are absent; neither sliver has corner glyphs. Single rendered regions in the other views also lack corner glyphs. The v2 top row y=22 contains both incoming heads at x=11 and x=43; the first head abuts the title. The title remains legible and the node borders remain intact. Region boundary/edge intersections are not route-route crossings. Pinned 77×47 dumps show the same defects.

The *two missing region labels themselves do not erase unique required topology facts*: the node text still says `virbr0 libvirt`, `docker0 172.18.2.1`, and `homelab ASUS Linux 7.0, ~/rack-bench Oct 11`, and the bridge and host links remain traceable. What is lost is the explicit `virtual nets` and `homelab host` grouping/title presentation, not the addresses or host identity. Do not count them as lost node identifiers.

**F4/F5 — source coverage, critical semantic defect / high category-fit defect.** The source says the Fios router combines ToR, spine, **border**, and firewall; v3 [18,7,48,1] reads `rtr = ToR+spine+FW, NAT+DHCP, pool 71.251.12.240`, which does not encode the border role. The documented abbreviation in generation.json cannot supply text a pane reader cannot see. The `Fios?` qualifier appears in v2, but the source's WNC OUI evidence is not rendered. The pool address appears, but that the whole home shares it does not. `2.5 GbE`, `backup`, metric 600 and `not ECMP` distinguish lanes, but there is no explicit primary-wired statement. `one ISP one path` is visible; the source's contrast to 4 ISP / 2 MMR is not. None should be silently credited just because the meaning ledger says drawn or calls the comparison a deliberate drop.

All explicitly requested numerical identifiers are visible: VM and container subnets, bridge address, two interface addresses and `/24`, gateway, Tailscale, shared-pool IP, ONT, aggregation, AS701, AS13335, and 1.1.1.1. `Fios?`, `hist`, `not ECMP`, `no v6 (ULA)`, and NAT / no committed rate / no v6 bench answers are present. Three historical WAN hops use dashed lines and all four WAN hop labels carry `hist`, qualifying the ONT claim; Fios is qualified by `?` in v2. The source prose puts 4.5/6.6/6–9 ms on the respective hop nodes, whereas the historical edge table puts those values on the following links. The capture follows the prose; it does not prove that those numbers are link latencies. This placement is documented, not treated as a reversed edge.

**F3 — v4 target identity, high defect.** At [20,38,1,1] the `bond0 eno1` arrow points to a box whose only label [20,40,45,1] is `bench internet: NAT, no committed rate, no v6`. The generation record describes that box both as the router and as an annotation, but the pane does not name it `rtr` or `192.168.1.1`; the reader cannot tell whether a new endpoint or the existing router is meant. The separate v2 bond-to-router arrow is correct, so the required relation is not missing. The extra apparent v4 relation is separately audited as failed semantics rather than claimed as an untraceable arrow.

**F6 — reproducibility, high evidence defect.** Checks for 12 candidates exist, but v2/v3/v4 first draft specs are explicitly reconstructed, not original byte-authoritative inputs; v1-a1's check was restored from a transcript. The raw check files do not record per-command invocation and exit status. The attempted group-layout probes are described, but the relevant first-draft frame captures are not preserved. This fails the frozen requirement to preserve rejected-draft raw command/status and probe logs. Final manifests supply check exit 0, artifact hashes, timestamp windows, pane IDs and cleanup. All recorded final/superseded manifest artifact SHA-256 values were independently recomputed and matched. The frozen skill, rubric, case manifest, case files and binary hashes also match round metadata.
