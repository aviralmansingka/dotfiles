## Regions, color and routing choices

**Renderer defect, not a failed fixed node-border gate:** all six shipped region outlines have plain horizontal glyphs in their four corner cells, rather than corner glyphs. The top, bottom and side runs still enclose the stated members; their colored and titled outlines remain readable. In v2 the outgoing routes overwrite three bottom-region cells. The hue-probe captures independently show sibling-region slivers displaced away from their members, a real renderer capability limitation. The shipped v1, v3 and v4 each use one multi-rank region and contain their members, while v2 uses single-rank sibling regions and leaves the gateway ungrouped. Thus the probe displacement is **evidence of a renderer limitation, not a shipped containment defect**. The generator's claim that every final view avoids multi-rank regions is not accurate: it avoids problematic multi-rank sibling combinations, not multi-rank single-region views.

On v2's title row the three regions measure (140,108,57), (150,90,51), and (110,118,66). Renaming `upstream hops` to the equivalent `external hops` to obtain a different measured hue is a legitimate choice here: both describe the same source-backed external path, and no semantic membership or order changes. Distinct colors across separate views are not a rubric requirement; do not treat the rename alone as a quality gain. The non-portable name-hash dependence is a renderer design concern, not evidence of arbitrary concept cycling here.

The no-trunk rationale is adequate per view: v1 is a sequential host chain; v2 has three distinct source-to-gateway links converging on separate arrowheads, not a one-source branching bus; v3 has two parallel annotations of the same boundary; and v4 is a sequential observed-hop chain.
