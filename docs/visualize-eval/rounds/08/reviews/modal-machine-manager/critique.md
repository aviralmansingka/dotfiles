## Findings: defects, blockers and evidence gaps

### Defects

1. **Critical — dropped source meaning.** The control-plane edge says only `create/term` [38,18,11,1], omitting repair; the webapi→queue edge says `reinstall`, omitting reprovision; webapi→Slack says `confirm`, omitting alerts; agent→public gRPC says `heartbeat`, omitting register. Queue→SSH says `reimage`, omitting audit/verify, while SSH→machines separately says `run audit`. Queue→S3 says `diagnose` without uploads and reconcile→providers says `gcore 1min` without sync. The generator explicitly records four deliberate omissions. These are not merely shorter typography when the missing words distinguish required actions.
2. **High — carried labels and owner.** In v2 the 77×47 `reimage` on row 27 survives adjacent to the queue→SSH route, although the audit/verify qualifier is absent. In v3 `pull image` on row 28 is associated with machines→S3, separate from queue→S3 diagnostics; the boot response on row 35 is visibly `boot.ipxe─+─S3 args` and runs into the region border, so its payload and route are not cleanly conveyed. Both iPXE directions are declared and have visible heads, but declaration is not a clean response trace.
3. **High — ambiguous owners.** Checks report one owner ambiguity in each of v1–v3 and none in v4. The generator explains an in-band multi-edge fan: sibling corridors sit close enough that the middle label cannot be strictly closer to its own corridor. The blind record gives independent examples and coordinates; the checks do not identify which individual labels they mean, so those examples are not presented as checker output. The generator records budget/space constraints, not proof that no unambiguous alternative exists. The ambiguity is therefore a defect, not an automatic waiver.
4. **High — region and glyph collision.** v1 jobs title is `──▼j▼bs ───────`; control/humans titles are absent. v2 [38,23,11,1] reads `janitor─app`, where a line replaces its space; the ops boundary crosses the janitor box. In v3 the boot-return text is overpainted. `groups:` membership and `labels: placed` do not establish intact visual membership or readable text. Source groupings are conceptually plausible; renderer geometry does not reliably express them.
5. **High — origin and corner.** At 77×47, v1 control-plane source's first drawn cell is (33,10), but the frame-0 dot is (33,11). v2 queue→registry first cell (18,22), dot (19,22); v3 machine outgoing first cells (28,18), (30,18), (32,18), dots on row 19; v4 agent first cell (7,10), dot (7,11). For v3 boot→machines, source port is (38,32), the first drawn route cell is (37,32), and frame-0 dot is at (36,32), a `╭` shown in later frames: it is a **non-origin corner**, not the allowed origin-corner exception. These are confirmed defects despite each check's `origins: N/N` and `0 violations`.
6. **High — lifecycle recipe not applied.** Pins report zero conflicts, but that only means the chosen pins are accepted. In v3 the three different boot/image/agent phases share a target rank and the boot.ipxe return travels upward around the region; the claimed sequence cannot be read top-to-bottom. v2 is intentionally left-to-right. Category fit requires the recipe in the visible output, not merely a family line or the presence of dots.

### Blockers and evidence gaps

- **Target-pane evidence gap / workflow defect:** every recorded actual pane header is `83x51 @100%`; manifests have null pane columns and 51 rows, and headers prove the wider dimension. The separate dumps state `viewport: 77x47 zoom: 1.00 natural`, but they are pinned dumps, not a measured live half-pane. A target-size pane cannot be inferred from a hardcoded environment. Therefore target live fit and full live target motion remain blocked, even though overpaint is already a known failure.
- **Full-lap live capture gap:** each live pane frames file retained 19 frames, not the requested 71; pinned target-sized dumps contain 71 frames at 100 ms. Correction to the immutable blind record: the v3 return dot goes from (36,32) to (34,36), a Manhattan distance of six cells, so its route advance cannot be five; the other sampled dots advance five. The blind record's five-cell claim for this route was arithmetic error. This is a measured rate discrepancy over the interval. Unequal arrivals are not a defect: same-arrival was not requested. The blind audit did not exhaustively identify every route's origin or every corner/bridge/arrowhead occupancy; known late origins and the non-origin corner stand regardless.
- **Reproducibility gap:** raw attempt outputs and route-probe files exist, but some candidate spec bytes were overwritten and raw files such as `attempts-raw/v3-a2-check.txt` contain only the error, not an exact command with distinct stdout, stderr and exit code. The failed v3 LR candidate reports `no obstacle-safe route for boot -> mch`; this proves that attempt failed, not that all possible layouts are impossible.
- **Border color evidence limit:** ANSI shows a gold selected border on 26/26 node occurrences in four simultaneous `--select-all` snapshots, not individual selections or uninterrupted boundaries at every crossing. The visible janitor collision already fails the gate.
