## Semantic and topology audit

The complete structured per-relation audit, including repeated OOB drops and extra/negative apparent links, is in `reviewer-result.json.edge_audit`. Key findings, classified:

- **Critical defect — controller coverage (F1).** Source original line 283 explicitly corrects the earlier diagram to show RMS direct control of compute BMCs and power-shelf controllers via Redfish/HTTPS. V1 [18,6,56,41] shows `nv-rms → OOB LAN` as a LAN client and then a physical bus; v2 repeats physical drops. Neither view identifies RMS's device API calls separately. V3 does preserve RMS→NVOS and RMS→NMX-C gRPC. The correction's shared controller is not restored by drawing only a shared wire.
- **High defect — trunk clarity (F2).** V1 [19,40,37,3] has `⌒` at [38,41], `┴` at [39,41], and heads [19,42], [38,42], [55,42]. One visible junction is necessary but not sufficient: the middle arrowhead immediately below a bridge looks like a crossing rather than a definite third branch. The left and right destinations are readable, but a reader must guess the middle attachment. `Redfish/HTTPS` [23,40,13,1] lies over the left arm and does not clearly own the whole bus. The check says `trunks: oob-bus from oob to bmc_c, 2 branches, 1 junctions` and warns that two cells including ports are shared. Intentional shared cells are appropriate for a bus; the failure is the actual ambiguous glyph arrangement, not the warning by itself.
- **Critical defect — air-gap direction (F3).** V2 [33,20,12,20] shows a dashed arrow from OOB to in-band with a target arrowhead. The source says the data plane is *usually* air-gapped and distinct, not that OOB sends traffic to it. A dashed line does not explain that it expresses separation or the word `usually`; this can mislead about exactly the network boundary the correction sought to clarify. The lack of OOB→DCGM is preserved across the captures.
- **High defect — residency and qualifiers (F4).** V3 [15,21,55,11] correctly shows an NVOS→NMX-C/T `hosts` arrow, RMS→NVOS with `NVUE/SSH/SFTP`, and OOB→NVSwitch BMC `power+serial`; however, the NVOS/NMX software is not shown residing on the NVSwitch tray. `REST` is missing from the NVUE label. V4 [23,22,38,26] does not show DCGM, driver/CUDA, and Fabric Manager on compute hosts. Mere absence of a GPU-die node does not establish host residency. DCGM→driver `NVML` is visible, but the reader is not explicitly told the read action or local/no-network qualifier.
- **Critical defect / capability blocker — unsupported Fabric Manager direction (F5).** V4 [39,36,22,12] draws driver+CUDA→Fabric Mgr, but `bundled` does not explain what the arrow means. The fixed source constraint requires supported read/monitor/host semantics or a block; a source-backed directed action was not established. Do not treat an unlabeled historical arrow as an independent factual oracle.
- **Medium defect — uncarried verbs/qualifiers (F6).** The GPU Operator→DCGM arrow plus `DCGM (DaemonSet)` does not explicitly say the operator deploys it. Exporter `:9400` is present, but the `metrics` label sits on DCGM→exporter rather than exporter→network, and Prometheus is not named. Ledger-only claims are not visible diagram coverage. The nv-rms source-build versus gated-image qualification is also only in the ledger.

Visible positive coverage: `Compute BMC×18` occurs in v1 and v2; CDU ctlr and PDUs are present and connected in v2; SSH/SFTP, NMX-C gRPC, and `hosts` are present in v3; Redfish/HTTPS is readable in v1 and v2, subject to the v1 ownership caveat. The requested paid-only products are absent; there is no OOB→DCGM or device-to-device link. The source correction supersedes the historical OOB→NVOS edge, so its absence is not counted as a missing edge.

## Family evidence and workflow

The generator's `generation.json` records this exact `family_evidence` string:

> The source is two weakly-connected worlds joined by one bus: an off-rack management plane (nv-rms as a client) and the OOB physical plant whose LAN reaches every device class, versus the in-band software world (Kubernetes -> GPU Operator -> DCGM -> dcgm-exporter / driver+CUDA -> Fabric Manager) that has no OOB edge at all. The source also demands the bus itself split once into three device classes, and never invents edges between the device classes ('they genuinely do not talk to one another').

The family line is preserved and source-motivated; its placement in generation metadata rather than the pane is not itself a workflow defect. However, characterizing the OOB bus as joining the OOB and in-band worlds conflicts with the no-OOB-to-in-band correction. More importantly, the chosen recipe requires the actual worlds as separate side-by-side regions, with a readable bus; the four split captures do not produce that layout or full cross-view coverage. The secondary `identity-probe-maps` recipe is named but several qualifiers remain only in the generator ledger.

## Correction candidates, not applied

- **Skill/generator:** distinguish physical shared OOB reach from RMS's separately directed device API relations; preserve positive/negative connectivity and `usually` without depicting separation as a data path. Cover host and NVSwitch-tray residency, action verbs, NVUE REST, Prometheus, and licensing qualifiers on the actual views, or mark the unsupported Fabric Manager action blocked rather than drawing an unqualified direction. Design cross-view identities and links so readers can reconstruct the whole without relying on a private ledger.
- **Renderer:** investigate the single-hub bus bridge/branch glyph collision, displaced singleton/multi-rank group rectangles, group-title overwriting, and label-space costs. The candidate's observations are real defects; its unpreserved calibration outputs do not establish that all possible alternative layouts are impossible. Avoid counting intentional bus port sharing as an independent-link collision, but still require a visibly traceable junction.
- **Harness/evidence:** preserve raw calibration commands and output, rejected draft bytes and hashes, every pane ID and closure proof, and a live actual 77×47 capture. Keep the direct-size dumps as complementary evidence, not a substitute for measured live geometry.
