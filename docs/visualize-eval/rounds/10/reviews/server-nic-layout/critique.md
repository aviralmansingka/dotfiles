## Semantic findings and rulings

1. **Critical defect — ungrounded cable observation.** v1 `no cable seen` [44,21,13,1], on a dashed route from `7 NICs dark` to server1, states a visual observation of no cable. The source only establishes dark interfaces and explicitly warns that an inactive interface does not prove no cable. A dashed line does not identify the origin of the purported observation or turn it into the correct unknown. Label the cable state unknown or explicitly identify it as an inference, not an observed absence. This affects required edge e1.
2. **High defect — missing OUI basis and hop-loss quantity.** v2 `gw .57 Arista? 0.1ms` [32,30,20,1] and the matching v3 gateway [32,10,20,1] preserve gateway role, address shorthand, 0.1 ms and vendor uncertainty, but `?` does not say the attribution rests on a MAC OUI, nor separate vendor identification from switch-role proof. v3 `3,6 silent, 5 lossy` [33,18,19,1] omits the stated **80%** loss, not merely a spelling variant. Both are source-backed qualifiers the generator marked as drawn and the identity recipe requires in a capture.
3. **High defect — identity and handoff facts lost.** `server1 R7725 103.19.91.58` [29,26,26,1] and `server2 R7725 .59` [34,21,17,1] provide the model but neither explicitly identifies Dell, PowerEdge, or bare metal. A model number alone does not state bare-metal deployment. `NxtGen AS132717 BLR` [44,13,19,1] appears on the egress span, not visibly as both machines' hosting relationship. Also `no NAT no v6` [23,24,12,1] does not entail the separate `no proxy` qualification. The ledger's claims that these facts are folded into R7725 and no NAT are not supported by what is shown.
4. **Ruling, no defect — address and abbreviations.** `103.19.91.58` appears in v1 and `same /29` in v2; reading across named repeated NIC views provides the prefix and makes gateway `.57`, server2 `.59`, and the pool suffixes recoverable. The full CIDR need not be repeated verbatim in each label. `25G`, `no v6`, `BLR`, `AirtelBB`, and `CF 1.1.1.1` are sufficiently recognizable in context. `free? unprobed` correctly qualifies the spare pool. Gateway role and RTT are explicit; the four-token gateway label is dense but readable, and compression itself is not the defect. `1.8 ms, no L2 proof` on the dashed path through silent hops adequately conveys the required missing-hop caveat without implying L2 is false. The exact 8.3–8.6 ms range is visible. No invisible switches are invented.
5. **Ruling, layout and motion.** All nine labelled edges use `label_slot`; the thin v1 is sparse but legible. The pinned final dumps show exact `viewport: 77x47 zoom: 1.00 natural` without overflow; live app headers report 82×51 @100%, not the target dimensions. All lone ports meet side midpoints within half-cell rounding; the gateway's three inputs bracket its midpoint. The blind coordinate audit and the checks agree: no crossings or ambiguous owners. Frame 0/1/2 graph content is identical at 0/100/200 ms, with no dots. No claimed motion cycle is needed for this source-backed optional-animation case.

## Category classification and recipe

In `generation.json` → `step0_classification`, the generator states `family: identity-probe-maps` and its evidence is: “The source is an identifier inventory, not a topology: one host's 29-bit address plan (103.19.91.58/29, gateway .57, .60-.62 free), a named NIC (eno16595np0 25 GbE), two Dell R7725 boxes, a ToR whose vendor comes only from a MAC OUI, and a probe caveat (hops 3 and 6 silent, hop 5 80% loss, 'a missing hop proves nothing about Layer 2').” Its tie-breaker 3 prioritizes qualifier density over flow-chain shape; this is supported. It records no secondary recipe, while the visible successive single-stage chains have room for corridors and no source-animated hops, so no additional motion requirement follows.

The ledger has named views, but the missing OUI basis, loss percentage, explicit manufacturer/host status and proxy qualification show that a ledger assertion is not proof of a capture. Measured abandoned 57×63 tb and 120×14 lr drafts support rejecting those two shapes; they do not prove that all possible faithful splits or longer labels are impossible, and do not justify dropping facts. The generator's `recipe_yield` itself says no fact was dropped to earn fit, which the captures contradict. The correct classification therefore fails the applied identity-map recipe.
