## Family, ordering, and recorded attempts

`generation.json`, `step0_classification`, states `family: service-meshes` and exactly: “modal-runtime is the core with a fan of three callees (modal-worker, Task engine, modal-daemon) and the source states an anti-parallel pair on the same two boxes: `worker -> runtime` task input + control RPCs and `runtime -> worker` events + management RPCs.” This family fits the source's three-way runtime fan and reciprocal pair. The generator states no secondary recipe as a design driver; v2 borrows flow-chain rhythm, and its two short left-to-right chains are visible.

The recipe requires source-listing fan order and two distinct **labelled** reciprocal edges. V1 indeed co-views the pair, but visual ownership is unresolved. At the callee rank the capture places `[modal-daemon, Task engine]`; the source relationship list has runtime → engine before runtime → daemon. `layers` pins the rank only, and the automatic engine group ordering chose the displayed reversal. This is a **medium-severity category-fit defect**, not evidence of a source-defined tier, and not a claim that the generator manually ordered the siblings. V2 puts backend before filesystem as listed. The four rank pins themselves are correctly honored and justified by the task-input reading direction; no `minlen` appears in the shipped specs.

Five abandoned shapes and the separate per-view drafts document useful measured attempts: pair-only bare LR has one ambiguous owner; slots group labels together; TB minlen skips a label; v2 without slots is 94 columns wide; other layouts have ambiguity or overflow. The generator explicitly says its pair `recipe_yield.yielded` is false. These limited probes do not establish the stronger rule's exception that **no unambiguous fitting placement existed**, nor do they demonstrate an order yield. Record the renderer limitation as a **capability blocker candidate**, not as a proved impossibility or an automatic waiver of failed categories. The checker treating reserved label bands as unambiguous despite closer neighboring routes is a renderer/checker correction candidate. Skill-level candidates are to inspect actual adjacency, preserve explicit action words and source port order, and avoid accepting the validator's ownership claim by itself.
