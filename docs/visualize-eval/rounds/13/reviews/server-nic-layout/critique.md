## Source and relation audit

The wide frame shows all eight historical relationships, with no extra apparent relationship. `dark → s1` and `spare → gw` **fail the semantic edge audit**, not the routing audit: `no cable seen` [23,14,13,1] implies a cable inspection that inactive NICs alone do not establish; `.60-.62 free` [75,20,12,1] plus `free, unprobed` [91,19,14,1] does not say free is inferred or uncertain. A dashed line does not by itself repair these statements. The brief explicitly asks to preserve historical qualifications, not tidy them into certainty.

The remaining six edges pass: `s1 → nic` only uplink; `nic → gw` default gateway 0.1 ms; `s2 → gw` same /29; `gw → airtel` 1.8 ms; `airtel → airtelbb` 7.1 ms; `airtelbb → cf` 8.3 ms. The 8.3 ms historical relation is explicitly specified by the brief even though source prose also says 8.6 ms; the generator records that discrepancy rather than inventing a resolution. The gateway appears in both diagrams as a seam, not an invented extra link. The Arista OUI caveat is present; no speculative spine/leaf box is drawn.

The source names NxtGen Cloud and gives full `103.19.91.58` and `.59` addresses; the drawing shows only `AS132717` and suffixes. An AS number without a visible key does not tell an audit reader the operator name or reconstruct the full prefix. The upstream description [9,27,114,1] says `Silent hops`, but it omits the numbered no-answer hops and 80% loss; the latter is partial loss, not silence. No invented device is needed to state those observations. The generator also drops the fresh-handoff status and Bengaluru, disclosing the decision; under the specific NIC-and-egress task those ancillary facts are less decisive than the named audit identifiers and trace qualifications.
