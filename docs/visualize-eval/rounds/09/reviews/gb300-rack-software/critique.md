## Semantic and edge audit

The full machine-readable audit is in `reviewer-result.json` (`edge_audit`, 18 entries). Source references are `docs/visualize-eval/cases/gb300-rack-software/source.md` and its fixed constraints, especially original lines 266, 271, 277, 283 and 284.

- **Critical defect F1:** RMS→Compute BMC, RMS→NVSwitch BMC and RMS→Power shelf are absent from all three captures. v1 `[15,18,49,14]` shows `nv-rms → OOB LAN → [three bus ends]`, not separate device API calls. v2 shows RMS→NVOS and RMS→NMX-C/T, not the three missing hardware calls. The source's explicit line 283 distinguishes shared controller from shared wire, so neither the bus nor the repeat anchor substitutes for the API fan. Missing edges fail even though renderer attempts are recorded.
- **Critical defect F2:** Redfish/HTTPS for the hardware controller API is absent, as are the explicit Prometheus `:9400` outbound detail and the `REST` qualification in `NVUE/SSH/SFTP`. The supplied source calls the physical OOB/data-plane air gap **usually** air-gapped. No OOB→DCGM line is correct and faithfully shows that specific negative relationship; a missing line between separate views does not itself express the qualified physical-network claim or its `usually` hedge. A textual qualifier would be needed to communicate the claim, but the fixed constraint does not demand a specific graphic boundary. The diagram does not state the gated-image caveat, but does not depict NGC or claim its image is free.
- **Bus passes:** v1 `[27,18,37,14]` starts at OOB LAN, reaches a single `┼` at `[45,24,1,1]`, and separates into distinct arrowheads at Compute BMC x18, NVSwitch BMC, and Power shelf. `--check` states `oob_bus from oob to bmc_c, 2 branches, 1 junctions`. Its shared-cell warning is a checker/renderer conflict at the intended bus junction, **not** an extra crossing or a generator fault. The minimal p4 probe also reports a shared-cell warning, though one overwritten earlier p4 run is unavailable. No additional line between endpoints appears.
- **Other drawn paths:** nv-rms→OOB LAN (client), nv-rms→NVOS (protocol label abbreviated), nv-rms→NMX-C/T (gRPC), NVOS→NMX-C/T (hosts), Kubernetes→GPU Operator (operator), GPU Operator→DCGM (DaemonSet), DCGM→dcgm-exporter (metrics), DCGM→Driver+CUDA (NVML read), and Driver+CUDA→Fabric Mgr (same host). The last label is shared residency, not an invented deployment or management dependency. The old OOB→NVOS historical line is not treated as an independent oracle: the later explanation separates the tray BMC's OOB drop from RMS→NVOS control.
- **Free software:** none of Mission Control, BCM, UFM, NetQ or gated NGC imagery is depicted. The last human prohibition is honored by the displayed node set. This does not repair omitted direct controller paths.
- **Other source coverage:** CDU and PDU endpoints, OOB payload details and the explicit free/bundled caveats are not drawn. The human specifically demanded exactly three bus ends, so adding bus branches just to list CDU/PDU would conflict with that shape. Separate readable annotation or a complementary view would be needed if those source details are to be represented without changing the trunk.

## Evidence, yields and limitations

The declared family line is in `generation.json` at `step0_classification`: `family: placement-planes`; its full evidence quote is preserved verbatim in the blind record and in the result. V1 has spatially separated off-rack and OOB portions, no group outline, and one splitting bus. V2 uses a vertical rather than side-by-side controller/software arrangement; v3 is a separate in-band view. This is a reasonable dominant classification, but the generator also explicitly claimed an identity-probe-map secondary recipe, whose capture coverage is incomplete. Ledger entries and drop reasons are useful records but do not make absent identifiers visible.

The proposed co-view yield has real, bounded evidence: v1-a1 and v1-a2 fail routing, as do the narrower compute-BMC and power-shelf API probes; p1's seven-node unlabelled topology routes but has four bridges and a shared run. The attempted configurations therefore support a renderer limitation for those configurations, not a mathematical proof that every layout or every possible additional API-only view is impossible. The v1-a3 log points to a mutable final-spec pathname, while its probe was reconstructed afterwards; similarly v3-a1's input was reconstructed. The raw outputs for these runs match their log hashes, but the originally executed input bytes are not independently frozen. Even a valid recipe yield cannot waive semantic fidelity, and the final views do not retain the direct hardware API edges elsewhere.

The v2 gRPC route is long: pinned frame 0 roughly `[32,19,14,18]`, leaving the controller leftward, traversing below NMX-C/T and returning to its top. The ranked alternative and LR alternative both failed routing in preserved records. The capture proves traceability, but those failures do not prove globally optimal routing; no better successful route is evidenced. This is an **unresolved capability question**, not a speculative avoidability defect. v1's intended junction warning is likewise an identified **checker-semantics conflict**; validator v1 honestly fails `check_warnings_absent`.

The three pinned dumps match their recorded stdout bytes and show `viewport: 77x47 zoom: 1.00 natural`. Each is tied by recorded command to its own final spec. The status header itself truncates at the right in v2/v3; graph labels and borders do not. `check.txt` says viewport unknown, so it is not the fit proof. Live app headers report 82×51; manifest pane rectangles are null. The node selections are proven by ANSI, not by plain text. The `--require-records` validators all fail line 8; v1 also reports its intentional shared-cell warning. Original overwritten raw bytes remain an **evidence gap**.

## Skill-level and renderer-level correction candidates

- **Skill/workflow:** preserve controller API calls as explicitly separate relationships in a complementary readable view when bus co-view routing fails; ensure the complete coverage map refers to captured edges, not only generator prose. Surface source-backed qualifiers and named residency tiers in the presentation. Do not claim an identity-map secondary recipe unless each required identifier and qualifier is visible. Generate unique record labels and archive immutable exact input bytes before each run.
- **Renderer/checker:** investigate constrained routes crossing the bus/rank gaps and the gRPC detour, without treating failed examples as proof of global impossibility. Distinguish a source-declared single bus junction from accidental shared corridor in diagnostics and validation. Preserve the semantic one-junction shape.
