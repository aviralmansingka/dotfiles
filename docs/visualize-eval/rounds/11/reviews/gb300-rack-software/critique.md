## Direct API and source relationship audit

The complete machine-readable audit is in `reviewer-result.json`. Relevant capture: `pinned/v3-v3-rms-api-a2-77x47.txt` frame 0. Independent tracing confirms nv-rms→Compute BMC (`Redfish/HTTPS` [19,22,13,1]), nv-rms→Power shelf (second `Redfish/HTTPS` [20,30,13,1]), nv-rms→NVOS (`NVUE REST/SSH` [33,22,13,1]), nv-rms→NMX-C/T (`NMX-C gRPC` [40,32,10,1]), and NVOS→NMX-C/T (`hosts` [49,23,5,1]). The bridge at [33,29,1,1] separates the Power shelf horizontal route from the descending gRPC route; it is not a junction. The visible `NVUE REST/SSH` does not explicitly preserve the source's `SSH/SFTP` qualifier. No direct nv-rms→NVSwitch BMC arrow is invented; the corrected source sends NVSwitch management through NVOS. No OOB→DCGM edge or BMC-to-BMC edge appears.

The ordinary source relations nv-rms→OOB, OOB→Compute BMC/NVSwitch BMC/Power shelf/NVOS, NVOS→NMX-C/T, Kubernetes→GPU Operator, GPU Operator→DCGM, DCGM→driver, and driver/Fabric Manager same-host relation have visible routes. The exporter route exists but its label is visually allocated next to the competing route. `same host` avoids asserting an unsupported deploy/manage interface. The absent CDU and PDU branches are separate critical source-coverage failures, not merely an unrecorded visual simplification.

The source says OOB is *usually* air-gapped, not always; the classification evidence retains that qualification. The drawing's disconnected planes demonstrate separation but do not establish an absolute air gap, LAN rates, switch pair, subnet, or physical hardware. Similarly, logical group name `host` is not rendered as a legend or location title; color alone cannot tell a reader that Driver/CUDA, DCGM and Fabric Manager reside on compute hosts. The diagram does not show gated NGC images or paid-only Mission Control/UFM/NetQ, and it does not claim gated images are free.

## Findings and disposition

- **F1 — critical defect:** CDU controller and PDU endpoints and OOB connections absent. Disclosed in `generation.json` `deliberate_drops`; the one-split demand is real, but complete coverage must be addressed rather than waived. Additional coordinated views or a non-arrow annotation might preserve the tension, but no compliant solution has been demonstrated here.
- **F2 — high defect:** three actual bus junctions rather than one, despite one source stem. The generator documents the frozen engine's spur-cap behavior; this is a plausible renderer constraint, not proof the fixed human requirement was satisfied.
- **F3 — high defect:** v4 [37,27,17,4] places `serves :9400` next to the right route while its declared owner is the left exporter branch. `check.txt` says zero ambiguous owners; visual evidence controls.
- **F4 — medium defect:** source-listed SFTP is absent from the visible `NVUE REST/SSH` label.
- **F5 — high defect:** semantic placement and key context, particularly management-node/compute-host residence, are not visibly identified. Group tags in a spec do not display explanatory names.
- **F6 — low localized defect:** v3's disclosed bridge reduces simplicity but remains traceable. Its avoidability is unresolved; it is not an independent gate failure here.
- **F7 — high evidence gap / blocker:** frozen dataset hash mismatch and lost rejected-draft bytes described above. The validators' green result does not establish byte reproducibility for an overwritten input.
