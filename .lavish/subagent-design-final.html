Subagent rendering — final design

Prototype C (inline chips + expandable trace) plus the existing blue-border live widget retained as the aboveEditor overview. Two surfaces, complementary roles — neither duplicates the other.

Coexistence, not replacement. The blue widget stays as the live fleet overview of running subagents and clears when nothing is running. The tree chips carry per-subagent lifecycle inline in the parent's reasoning flow and persist as a permanent, expandable record. The widget answers "what's running now"; the chips answer "how does this subagent fit my reasoning, and what did its tools do."
