V01

Does expanded projection remain exact, bounded, and current?

● PASS

What it proves

V01 proves deterministic exact geometry at 160×48 and 120×32, safe bounded rows and cells, chronological RFC3339 ordering with stable ties, retained latest evidence and participant context, and unchanged compact-model semantics.

What it leaves out

V01 leaves out live key handling, PTY resize recovery, and whether lifecycle states remain distinct across the read-only authority boundary; those are V02 and V03.

V02

Do actual model and PTY interactions survive every size boundary?

● PASS

What it proves

V02 proves actual atlas.NewExpandedModel(...) behavior through the real CLI PTY: j/k selection, Enter detail collapse, the 160×48→120×32→119×31→160×48 sequence, exact diagnostic output, recovery of selection and collapse state, clean quit, and immutable state, vault, and home manifests with no orchestration trap calls.

What it leaves out

V02 leaves out canonical writer behavior and does not claim mutation authority. It also does not replace V01’s byte-for-byte static golden comparison or V03’s complete lifecycle vocabulary check.

V03

Does the board preserve lifecycle meaning without taking authority?

● PASS

What it proves

V03 proves eleven distinct lifecycle kind/state projections—including awaiting, resuming, active phases, blocked, failed, and unknown—render deterministically and without ANSI, while registry, vault, and home manifests remain unchanged and all writer/orchestration traps remain untouched.

What it leaves out

V03 leaves out lifecycle transitions, resume execution, feedback handling, cleanup execution, and all canonical writes. The Operations Board observes authority; it does not exercise or acquire it.
